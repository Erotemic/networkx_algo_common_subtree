## Printed Definition 2 comparison (new, build pending)

`PaperContainment.lean` defines `PrintedContains` as the least inductive
relation generated by the two printed clauses of Definition 2. Its proposed
Lean proofs establish:

- `PrintedContains.semilength_le` and `eq_of_semilength_eq`;
- context-closure of `DyckContractStep` / `DyckEmbeds`;
- concatenation of independent containment witnesses;
- `PrintedContains.toDyckEmbeds` (soundness of the literal definition);
- `PrintedContains.toTreeEmbedding` (soundness for the tree relation);
- a semilength measure for Dyck contexts and strictly decreasing deletion.

**Not compiled in this environment.** Run `lake build` locally and inspect
all errors and warnings. No `sorry`, `admit`, or new axioms are intentionally
present in the source. The new module also proposes a positive Lean proof of the concrete
`001011 → 0011` contraction. The negative assertion that the literal
`PrintedContains` excludes this pair remains checked by independent finite
Python models, but has not yet been proved in Lean.
See `docs/planning/lozano_valiente_2004_containment_definition_audit.md`.
